# Reduced-Fee Eligibility Statement v1 (Vedat LLC)

**Applies to:** the reduced-fee ($195) license of the Lockheed Martin Federal Contract Awards by Corporate Family, FY2023–FY2025 data packet (FCA-CF v1.0), and to later Vedat LLC packets whose checkout names this statement.
**Licensor:** Vedat LLC
**Works with:** the packet's buyer terms (FCA-CF v1.0, docs/LICENSE.md). This statement adds to those terms. It does not change them.

## 1. Who qualifies

You may buy at the reduced fee only if you are buying for, and will use the packet within, one of these:

1. **A newsroom.** This is an organization whose main work is publishing news or journalism to the public. It also covers a journalist, including a freelancer, who is working on a specific assignment for such an organization.
2. **A nonprofit.** This is an organization recognized as tax-exempt under section 501(c)(3) of the U.S. Internal Revenue Code, or an organization with equivalent charitable status in its own country.
3. **An academic.** This is a student, faculty member, or researcher at a degree-granting college or university who is doing non-commercial research or teaching.

The reduced fee does not cover use for a commercial client, consulting, lobbying for hire, investment, or business development, even if the buyer also belongs to one of the groups above. That use needs the commercial license.

## 2. How eligibility is checked

You ask for the reduced fee by writing to info@vedat.io. Vedat LLC may check your request by your work email address, a public nonprofit or tax-exempt record, your school affiliation, or other reasonable evidence. If Vedat LLC approves, it sends you a personal checkout link. Vedat LLC may decline a request without giving a reason. Declining never stops you from buying the commercial license.

## 3. What you confirm

By accepting this statement at checkout, you confirm that what you told Vedat LLC about yourself and your organization is true, and that you will use the packet only as section 1 allows.

## 4. If a claim was not true, or your use changes

If you did not qualify when you bought, or you later use the packet for a purpose section 1 excludes, you owe Vedat LLC the difference between the commercial fee and the fee you paid ($300 for FCA-CF v1.0). Vedat LLC will ask for it in writing. If you do not pay within 30 days of that request, your license to the packet ends.

Write to Vedat LLC at info@vedat.io.
